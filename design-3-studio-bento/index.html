Straight answers

Questions, answered.

Do we own the store and all customer data?

100%. You retain full ownership of the domain, storefront code, product database and customer list with zero vendor lock-in.

Can you connect the storefront to our ERP, CRM or accounting tools?

Yes. We connect the storefront directly with your ERP, CRM or accounting tools (like Tally or Zoho) so inventory, orders and customer data stay synced in real time.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, we sign NDAs before analysing any proprietary ideas or onboarding new projects.

How do you handle payments on e-commerce platforms?

We integrate trusted PCI-compliant payment gateways like Stripe, Razorpay or PayPal, so customers’ checkout info never directly touches your servers.
