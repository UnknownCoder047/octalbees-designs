QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Who owns the source code and intellectual property?

You do — 100%. Every repository, design asset, database schema, and cloud configuration built for your project is handed over directly to your team with zero vendor lock-in.

How quickly can I get a project scope and estimate?

After a free 30-minute discovery call, our in-house engineers send a clear scope, architecture plan, timeline, and milestone pricing within 48 hours.

Why build a custom ERP or CRM instead of paying for SaaS tools?

Off-the-shelf tools charge per user every month and rarely match your exact approval, warehouse, or GST invoicing workflows. A custom Octalbees ERP/CRM fits how your team actually works — with zero per-seat fees.

Do you work with international teams outside India?

Yes. We actively build and support e-commerce stores, custom software, and technical SEO platforms for teams across the UK, Germany/EU, and India — including multi-currency Stripe & Razorpay setups.

What happens after my website or software launches?

Every delivery includes 30 days of complimentary engineering support covering QA, tuning, and team onboarding. After that, you can run it independently or partner with us for growth sprints.
